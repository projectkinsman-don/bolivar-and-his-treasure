# ADR 0004: Strict Relative Scale & Proportions Lock (Big Papa vs. Bolívar)

- **Status:** Approved
- **Date:** 2026-08-18
- **Context:** An inconsistency in the relative size of Bolívar (English Mastiff) compared to Big Papa was identified across different scenes (e.g. Page 1 sofa scene vs. Cover vs. Reunion scene). For a children's book centered on a "BIG dog", scale consistency is paramount.
- **Decision:** Lock a 3-point proportional scale rule:
  1. **Standing on All Fours:** Bolívar's back/withers reaches Big Papa's **belt/hip line** (~32 inches high), and his head reaches Big Papa's **mid-chest**.
  2. **Sitting Upright:** Top of Bolívar's head reaches Big Papa's **chest/shoulder level**.
  3. **Standing on Hind Legs / Hugging:** Top of head is **eye-level or taller than Big Papa** (~6'0" equivalent).
  4. **Mass & Bulk:** Bolívar's head must be substantial (heavy square skull, thick neck, wide chest ~2x the width of Big Papa's torso).
- **Consequences:** All future page prompts and generation references must explicitly enforce these proportional landmarks to ensure zero scale drift across all 54+ pages.
