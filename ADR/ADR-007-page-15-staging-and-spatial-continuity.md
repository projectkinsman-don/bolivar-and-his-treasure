# ADR-007: Page 15 (Scene 06B) Staging & Spatial Continuity

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Page 15 (Scene 06B) introduces Bolívar presenting his favorite toy, the Red Raggedy Stuffed Tiger (2D_MS_03), to Big Papa while Big Papa sips his morning coconut coffee. Scene 7 (Pages 16–17 Spread) immediately follows with Bolívar bolting up the staircase onto the master bed for a game of tug-of-war.

## Decision
We adopt **Option 3: Staircase Threshold / Launchpad Staging** for Page 15:
1. **Environment:** Hallway base by the mahogany staircase with modern horizontal metal railings (2D_ENV_01 Panel 2).
2. **Big Papa:** Seated near the staircase threshold with his steaming coconut coffee mug, looking down affectionately at Bolívar with an amused 'Oh no, Bolívar, not right now' expression.
3. **Bolívar:** Facing 3/4 rightward toward Big Papa in an energetic, ready-to-bolt playful stance. Anatomical right cheek is visible, strictly showing his small subtle beauty-mark mole.
4. **Prop:** The Red Raggedy Stuffed Tiger is dropped on the hardwood floor right across Big Papa's slippers.
5. **Spatial Bridge:** Establishing the staircase in the background creates clear spatial continuity when Bolívar dashes up those exact stairs on Pages 16–17.

## Consequences
- Readers visually anticipate the upstairs race before turning the page.
- Direct alignment with 2D_ENV_01 Panel 2 and locked model sheets.
