# ADR-004: Master 3D Environmental Architecture and 4-Language Cultural Matrix

## Status
**Accepted & Locked** (2026-09-02)

## Context
The children's picture book project *Bolívar and His Treasure* by Benjamin Kiamco requires 20 full-color sequential story scenes across 4 language editions (English `EN`, Indonesian `ID`, Tagalog `TL`, Spanish `ES`). To prevent background inconsistencies and streamline multi-language asset production, a structured 3D spatial layout and modular cultural matrix must be established.

## Decision
1. **Spatial Layout Standardization:**
   - **Zone 1 (Interior):** Open floor plan connecting Living Room (sofa/coffee table) -> Stairs & White Pantry Door -> Kitchen Island (marble counter, bone-handle WOOF jar, runner rug) -> Upstairs Bedroom (master bed).
   - **Zone 2 (Exterior Neighborhood):** Red brick townhouse stoop -> Sidewalk with Pizzeria -> Sports Court -> High-rise with Landmark Topper -> Hydrant & Pothole.
   - **Zone 3 (The Great Park & Downtown):** Open park field & grove -> Downtown urban core with towering skyscrapers and heavy traffic.

2. **Modular Cultural Swapping Matrix:**
   - **Sports Court:** Sand Volleyball (`EN`, `ID`, `ES`) vs. Basketball Court (`TL`).
   - **Building Topper:** Green Shamrock (`EN`, `ES`) vs. Kupu-Kupu Butterfly (`ID`) vs. Sampaguita Flower (`TL`).
   - **Park Field:** Baseball Diamond (`EN`) vs. Soccer Field (`ID`, `ES`) vs. Open Meadow Damuhan (`TL`).
   - **Pizzeria:** Flags removed across all editions.

3. **Visual Standard:**
   - Full-bleed 3D CGI animation aesthetic matching *Babar and the Adventures of Badou*, soft volumetric lighting, rich color saturation, zero blank margins.

## Consequences
- Guarantees 100% architectural continuity across all 20 story scenes.
- Enables clean, efficient plate-swapping for non-English editions without re-illustrating entire scenes from scratch.
