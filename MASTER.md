## 🔤 Master Typography & Typesetting Standards (ADR-006 Locked)

* **Interior Story Narration Font:** Strictly **Quicksand Bold** for all interior narration text.
* **Uniform Narration Font Size:** All narrative text is typeset at the identical font size across all pages.
* **Italicization Rule:** When italics are used (e.g., *They are going for a walk to the Big Park.*), the font size **must remain exactly the same** as the non-italicized body text (no size scaling).
* **Cover Title Font:** **Baloo 2 Extra Bold** in uniform warm golden-yellow (`#FFD700`).
* **Title Accent Spelling:** Strictly spelled with the Spanish acute accent: **"Bolívar and His Treasure"**.
* **Author Name Format:** Strictly **"Benjamin Kiamco"** (centered at bottom, no "by").
