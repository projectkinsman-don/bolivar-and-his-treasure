# 3D Environmental Architecture & Location Guide
## *Bolívar and His Treasure* — Author: Benjamin Kiamco

This master guide establishes the 3D environmental architecture, spatial layouts, and cultural asset variations across all 4 language editions (English `EN`, Indonesian `ID`, Tagalog `TL`, Spanish `ES`).

---

## 🏡 Zone 1: Townhouse Interior (Living Spaces)

### 1.1 Living Room & Foyer (`3D_LOC_01A`)
* **Key Props & Furniture:**
  * Comfortable plush fabric sofa (warm neutral/grey) where Big Papa sits drinking coconut coffee.
  * Wooden coffee table holding Big Papa's coffee mug (white ceramic mug with sweet coconut coffee aroma).
  * Hardwood flooring with a soft textured living room area rug.
  * Warm ambient lighting through large living room windows.
* **Story Scenes:** Scene 1 (Intro / Big Hugs), Scene 6 (Coffee sipping & tiger toy drop), Scene 20 (Ending Spread / Greatest Treasure).

### 1.2 Pantry & Interior Wooden Stairs (`3D_LOC_01B`)
* **Key Architecture:**
  * Open wooden staircase with white balusters and rich oak steps leading upstairs to the bedroom.
  * White paneled **Pantry Door** positioned at the base of the stairs where treats/cookies are stored.
* **Story Scenes:** Scene 2 (Morning race down stairs barking "Woof!"), Scene 7 (Racing upstairs to bed).

### 1.3 Kitchen Island & Feeding Station (`3D_LOC_01C`)
* **Key Architecture:**
  * Modern kitchen island with white/grey marble countertop.
  * **"WOOF" Cookie Jar:** Glossy white ceramic canister with sculpted dog-bone handle on lid, placed on the island counter.
  * **Kitchen Rug:** Patterned runner rug where the tossed cookie lands ("Plop!").
  * **Water Bowl Station:** Stainless steel water bowl on a silicone mat near the kitchen cabinets ("Slurp! Slurp!").
* **Story Scenes:** Scene 3 (Drooling at cookie jar), Scene 4 (First cookie toss on rug), Scene 5 (Mid-air cookie catch), Scene 6 (Water bowl slurp).

### 1.4 Upstairs Bedroom & Master Bed (`3D_LOC_01D`)
* **Key Architecture:**
  * Large king-size bed with fluffy white/navy bedding and pillows.
  * Hardwood floor, bedside nightstands.
* **Story Scenes:** Scene 7 (Tug-of-war with red raggedy tiger on bed).

## 🏙️ Zone 2: Townhouse Exterior & Neighborhood Sidewalk

### 2.1 Brick Townhouse Facade & Stoop (`3D_LOC_02`)
* **Architecture:**
  * Classic warm red brick townhouse facade with white window trim and dark front door.
  * Clean concrete front steps (stoop) where Big Papa stands with his coffee waiting for Bolívar.
  * Tall black wrought-iron fence and tidy green sidewalk lawn strip.
* **Story Scenes:** Scene 8 (Clipping leash at door), Scene 9 (Leaving townhouse), Scene 18 & 19 (Reunion on front stoop).

### 2.2 Corner Pizzeria (`3D_LOC_03A`)
* **Design Rule:** Clean Italian pizzeria signage, outdoor seating with awning, brick exterior. *(Note: Remove flags per Slide 19 instructions).*
* **Story Scenes:** Scene 9 (Passing pizzeria on walk), Scene 17 (Smelling pepperoni, mozzarella, and tomato on way home).

## 🌳 Zone 3: The Great Park & City Perimeter

### 3.1 The Great Park & Sports Fields — Cultural Matrix (`3D_LOC_04A`)
| Edition | Playing Field | Visual Description |
|---|---|---|
| **English (`EN`)** | **Baseball Diamond** | Dirt baseline, home plate, manicured grass outfield. |
| **Spanish (`ES`)** | **Cancha de Fútbol** | Green soccer pitch, white goalposts and penalty box lines. |
| **Indonesian (`ID`)** | **Lapangan Bola** | Green soccer pitch, white goalposts. |
| **Tagalog (`TL`)** | **Damuhan (Open Meadow)** | Lush expansive open green grass park field with trees. |

### 3.2 Park Playground & Grove (`3D_LOC_04B`)
* **Landmarks:** Large shady oak/maple trees, wooden park benches, swings in distance, open green rolling hills.
* **Story Scenes:** Scene 11 (Meeting Laila & Odin), Scene 12 (Spotting the chocolate Lop bunny), Scene 15 (Big Papa searching at sunset).

### 3.3 Downtown / Lost in the City (`3D_LOC_04C`)
* **Atmosphere:** Towering glass and steel skyscrapers, multi-lane road with heavy car traffic, absence of green trees, cool blue/grey urban lighting.
* **Story Scenes:** Scene 14 (Bolívar lost on sidewalk, tears falling "Splat! Splat! Splat!").
