### Proportional Scale Benchmarks:
1. **Big Papa (Standing Height):** **6 ft / 180 cm**
2. **Bolívar on All Fours:** Back withers at **32 in / 80 cm** (hip level), head top reaches **4 ft / 120 cm** (mid-chest to Big Papa).
3. **Bolívar Sitting Upright:** Head reaches **4.5 ft / 135 cm** (shoulder level to Big Papa).
4. **Bolívar on Hind Legs (Hugging):** Head reaches **6 ft / 180 cm** (eye-to-eye with Big Papa).

## 👨 Main Character: Big Papa (Owner)

| Feature | Specification | Strict Consistency Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Likeness & Build** | Adult man, early 40s, athletic/sturdy build, height ~6'0" | Maintain consistent adult proportions across all scenes. |
| **Complexion & Face** | Warm tan/olive skin tone, friendly wide smile, warm brown eyes | Keep facial features and friendly smile consistent. |
| **Hair** | Short dark brown/black hair with clean high fade on sides | Do not change haircut or hair color. |
| **Wardrobe** | Light blue short-sleeve button-down shirt, navy trousers, brown casual shoes | Standard outfit across daytime scenes (unless story specifies casual t-shirt). |

## 🐕 Main Character: Bolívar (English Mastiff)

| Feature | Specification | Strict Consistency Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Breed & Exact Scale** | Purebred English Mastiff. **SCALE LOCK:** On all fours, back reaches Big Papa's hip/belt and head reaches mid-chest. When sitting, head reaches Big Papa's shoulders. When on hind legs, head reaches eye-level/taller than Big Papa. | **MANDATORY:** Bolívar must consistently be massive and bulky relative to Big Papa across every single scene. |
| **Coat & Color** | Warm fawn/apricot short smooth coat with soft watercolor texture | Keep warm fawn hue consistent across lighting conditions. |
| **Muzzle & Mask** | Distinctive black muzzle/mask covering the snout, dark wrinkles on forehead, droopy jowls | Black mask is mandatory on all facial angles. |
| **Ears & Eyes** | Dark floppy ears hanging downward, kind expressive dark eyes with soft highlights | High emotional expressiveness (curiosity, joy, excitement). |
| **Tail & Movement** | Long tail with gentle upward curl; heavy, joyful gallop | Expressive tail wagging during happy scenes. |

## 🎨 Background & Environmental Consistency

1. **Art Technique:** 2D ink line art filled with warm watercolor textures. Soft, painterly backgrounds that don't overpower the characters.
2. **Color Palette:** Warm, sunlit yellows, sky blues, grassy greens, and earthy brick tones mirroring the classic *Curious George* aesthetic.
3. **Perspective:** Ground-level camera angles suitable for picture books, keeping focal characters clear and uncluttered.
