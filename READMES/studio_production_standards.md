## 🎭 2. Facial Expression & Acting Guidelines

To keep expressions lively and on-model without distorting character geometry:

### **Bolívar (The English Mastiff):**
1. **Adoring & Sweet (Page 1):** Soft almond-shaped eyes looking upward, closed gentle mouth with loose jowls, ears resting relaxed against head.
2. **Anticipation & Drool (Page 3):** Wide circular sparkling eyes with white catchlights, perked forward floppy ears, mouth slightly open showing pink tongue tip and a comic watercolor drool droplet.
3. **High Action & Catch (Page 5):** Jaws wide open to catch the cookie, floppy ears streaming straight back, dynamic speed lines.
4. **Curious Sniffing (Page 6):** Snout tilted upward at 30 degrees, nostrils flared, whimsical golden coffee aroma trails swirling into nose.
5. **Reunion Bliss (Page 8):** Eyes closed in happy curved crescents `(⌒‿⌒)`, mouth open delivering a huge sloppy lick across Big Papa’s cheek.

### **Big Papa (The Owner):**
1. **Relaxed Affection (Page 1):** Gentle smile, soft brow, warm eye wrinkles.
2. **Playful Energy (Pages 4–5):** Animated wide grin, arm extended in a fluid underhand throw.
3. **Relief & Joy (Pages 7–8):** Open mouth shouting with happiness, eyes squeezed shut in joyful laughter during the big hug.

## 🎬 3. Cinematic Staging & Visual Flow Rules

### **Rule 1: Left-to-Right Narrative Momentum (The "Page-Turn" Rule)**
In children's picture books, Western readers read left to right. Characters moving toward their goal should travel **from left to right across the page**:
- Bolívar galloping down the stairs moves **left-to-right**.
- Big Papa tossing cookies throws from **left to right**.
- Bolívar turning the volleyball court corner travels **left-to-right toward home**.

### **Rule 2: Camera Distance Variety**
To keep a 54+ page book visually engaging, never repeat the same camera shot twice in a row:
- **Wide Establishing Shot (Full Scene):** Volleyball court neighborhood, front of townhouse.
- **Medium Two-Shot:** Big Papa and Bolívar interacting on sofa / kitchen island.
- **Dynamic Low-Angle Shot:** Bolívar airborne catching cookie / sprinting down stairs.
- **Intimate Close-Up:** Big Papa hugging Bolívar / cookie jar detail.

## 🏺 4. Prop Consistency Protocol

- **"WOOF" Jar:** Always cylindrical white ceramic with uppercase black serif "WOOF" and sculpted bone handle. Never swap for a glass jar or plain tin.
- **Cookies:** Always golden round sandwich cookies with embossed circular patterns. Never render as dog bones.
- **Coffee Mug:** Always white ceramic mug with steam curls (coconut coffee scent).
